## Overall Impression

The welcome screen has useful student-focused actions and keyboard shortcuts, but it explains too much in competing blocks before making the first useful step unmistakable. The largest opportunity is to simplify the flow around opening a course file, then introduce Vault setup only when it helps.

## What’s Working

- The actions map to real tasks—opening a file, making a note, and researching—rather than generic feature promotion.
- Visible keyboard shortcuts help students move quickly once they know what they need.
- Recent files and bookmarks support returning to an active study session.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Repeated onboarding actions** — “Start here” and a later “First steps” section repeat the same four actions. That makes the screen longer without helping students choose. **Fix:** Consolidate into one short quick-start section, with file opening as the primary action and shortcuts as supporting details. **Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`.
- **[P1] Vault search lacks a first-use path** — A new student may see search but have no Vault configured; an empty result can look like failure. **Fix:** Explain what Vault search covers and offer “Add a course folder” when no Vault exists; show a useful no-results state. **Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`.
- **[P1] Welcome content may be unreachable in compact windows** — The page has substantial vertical spacing and lower sections, but no apparent scroll container. **Fix:** Ensure the welcome content remains reachable at short window heights using a scroll area or compact layout. **Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt`.
- **[P2] Labels should match product language and behavior** — “Zero-telemetry document workstation” foregrounds technical/privacy language, and an action label may not precisely describe whether it opens a tab or panel. **Fix:** Lead with the study-workspace benefit and make each label match its actual action. **Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`.

## Persona Red Flags

- **First-time student without a Vault:** Search looks ready but may return nothing, with no clear folder-setup path.
- **Student on a small laptop:** A long, unscrollable welcome layout may hide lower content such as recent files or bookmarks.
- **Student returning under deadline:** Duplicate onboarding blocks make it take longer to scan down to their recent work.

## Minor Observations

- The search placeholder combines Vault-file search and URL browsing, which are distinct tasks and may need clearer guidance.
- The search path may be limited to a small number of filename matches; the welcome screen does not communicate that scope.
- The welcome content is capped at 800 px, so its fit at narrower window sizes merits verification.

## Questions to Consider

- Is the welcome screen’s first job to explain privacy or get a student into their course material?
- Should students need to understand Vaults before being shown how to find their files?
- If the same four actions appear twice, which one should they trust?
