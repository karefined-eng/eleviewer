Welcome to EleViewer: The Sovereignty Workstation 🐘

> "Knowledge is only powerful when it is accessible, structured, and entirely under your control."

Welcome to EleViewer, a minimalist, blazing-fast study workspace designed to get out of your way and let your mind work. It was built with a core philosophy: Data Sovereignty. Your notes, your PDFs, your data—they all live locally on your machine. Every file your professor throws at you. One free app. Everything a student needs to read, write, and organize coursework — and nothing that slows an old laptop down. No telemetry, no forced cloud accounts, and no loading screens.

The Story Behind the Build

EleViewer wasn't built in a corporate boardroom. It was forged in the fire of academic necessity at the University of Ghana. We realized that modern productivity tools were bloated, slow, and constantly trying to lock us into their cloud ecosystems.

We needed a tool that was:
Lightweight: Fast enough to run on any student laptop.
Unified: Capable of reading PDFs, editing Markdown, and crunching CSVs in one clean interface.
Private: No mandatory telemetry, no forced cloud syncs.

Thus, the Sovereignty Workstation was born.

## 📂 Getting Started: Setting Up Your Vault

The first thing you need is a place to store your course materials.
1. Press `Alt + V` or click the Folder icon on the left to open the **Vault Explorer**.
2. At the top of the sidebar, click the **Settings icon** (⚙️) or press `Alt + S`.
3. In the Settings dialog, under **Vault Configuration**, add the folders where your study materials (PDFs, PPTXs, Notes) live.
4. Your files will instantly appear in the sidebar, fully searchable!

*Pro Tip:* While in Settings, check out the **Theme** section to change the app's Accent Color to match your style.

## ⚡ Master the "REFLEX" Keys

To truly unlock EleViewer's speed, you must master the core shortcuts. Keep your hands on the keyboard and let the workstation flow with your thoughts:

**Ctrl + Q : The Quick Switcher**
Never reach for the mouse to find a file. Press `Ctrl + Q` to instantly pull up the search overlay, type a few letters of the file you need, and press Enter.

**Alt + V : Toggle Vault Sidebar**
Need more screen space? Press `Alt + V` to collapse or expand the left-hand folder view. Perfect for deep focus.

**Ctrl + T : New Web Panel**
Researching online? Don't break your context. Press `Ctrl + T` to open a lightweight web browser directly alongside your notes.

**Ctrl + Shift + T : Reopen Closed Tab**
Accidentally closed a crucial reading? Bring it right back exactly where you left off.

**F9 : Read Aloud (Universal TTS)**
Turn any lecture slide, PDF, or Markdown file into audio. Highlight text or press `F9` to listen while you rest your eyes.

**Alt + E : System-Wide Quick Note**
Need to jot something down while in another app? Press `Alt + E` from anywhere in Windows to instantly summon a scratchpad.

**F1 : Getting Started Guide**
Press `F1` anytime to re-open this guide.
