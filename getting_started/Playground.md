# Welcome to EleViewer

EleViewer is your unified workspace for studying and research. Instead of a boring tutorial, let's learn by doing!

## 1. The Global Quick Note
Press `Alt+E` right now.
*(You'll see a scratchpad appear instantly. You can do this from anywhere in Windows!)*

## 2. Drop a Bookmark
Highlight this text and press `Ctrl+D` (or click the Bookmark icon in the toolbar).
*(Notice how the bookmark icon on the left updates. You can jump right back here later.)*

## 3. Split-Screen Web
Press `Ctrl+T` to open the web panel (if it isn't already).
*(Search for a topic while keeping this document open.)*

## 4. Zoom the Web
Click into the web panel and press `Ctrl + +` to zoom in.

## 5. Search Your Vault
Press `Ctrl+Q` to quickly search your files.
*(Instantly jump between documents without taking your hands off the keyboard.)*

## 6. Customize Your Space
Press `Alt+S` to open your Settings.
*(Head to the Web Browser Panel tab to find advanced options like hardware acceleration and context menus.)*

---
*Once you've tried these, you're ready to start studying! You can close this tab anytime.*
