# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Windows desktop (PySide6)

## Users

Students keeping readings, assignments, slides, and notes together during study.

## Product Purpose

EleViewer is a local-first study workspace that helps students keep their course materials and notes together in one place. Success means students can read, research, and capture notes without constantly switching between separate apps.

## Positioning

EleViewer combines local document reading, side-by-side web research, and persistent study tools in one Windows app. Its defining advantage is the connected study workspace, not any single file viewer.

## Operating Context

Students use EleViewer on Windows 10 or 11 to open local course files, link school folders as searchable Vaults, and keep research beside the material they are reading. They can restore a study session, save bookmarks, and open a Quick Note with a system-wide shortcut.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Opens PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, CSV, Markdown, HTML, and text files locally.
- Includes a web panel for intentional online research, live search across linked folders, persistent bookmarks, session restore, and a system-wide Quick Note.
- User documents stay on the user's device, and the app remains usable without an internet connection. Online access is limited to features that require it, such as web browsing, update checks, and crash or feedback reports the user chooses to send.
- The product is designed to stay lightweight and avoid requiring an administrator account or a Microsoft Office subscription.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the name EleViewer.
- Keep user-facing language direct, conversational, and accessible to a middle-school reading level.
- Avoid developer jargon in user-facing copy.

## Product Principles

1. Keep user documents local and usable without an internet connection.
2. Bring reading, research, and note-taking into one focused study workspace.
3. Reduce friction in common study tasks without unnecessary weight or complexity.
4. Use clear language that students can understand quickly.
