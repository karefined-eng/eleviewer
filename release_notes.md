# EleViewer v1.3.3: Welcome Screen Redesign & Better Bookmarks

Welcome to EleViewer v1.3.3! This update brings a massive overhaul to how you start your sessions and significantly expands the built-in browser's bookmarking capabilities.

## ✨ Highlight: Split-Screen Web Panel & Persistent Bookmarks
*We've upgraded the web panel so your research is always right where you left it.*

- **Interactive Bookmarks Bar:** The web panel now features a dedicated bookmarks bar and a full Bookmark Manager dialog. 
- **Export & Import:** You can now export and import your bookmarks and cookies, making it incredibly easy to backup your study sessions or migrate them to another machine.
- **Better Fullscreen:** Improved the fullscreen web handling so you can read articles without any distractions.

---

## 📚 Workspace & Vaults
*Updates to how you manage and find your local documents.*

- **The New Omnibar:** We completely redesigned the Welcome Screen! It now features a central "Omnibar" that lets you instantly search your local Vault files or enter a web URL to start browsing immediately upon launching the app.
- **Quick Access Columns:** The new welcome screen gives you one-click access to your recent files and persistent bookmarks directly on startup.

---

## 🛠️ General Improvements & Fixes
- **Smaller App Footprint:** We optimized the compilation pipeline to strip out unused libraries, making your installed app slightly smaller and faster.
- **UI Polish:** Standardized toolbar button sizes across the entire application for a cleaner look.
- **Stability:** Fixed a rare crash that could occur when dragging and dropping files.
- **Under the Hood:** Resolved an internal issue with our automated build pipeline to ensure patches are shipped reliably.
